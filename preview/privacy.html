Privacy Policy for FitTrack

Last updated: September 17, 2026

FitTrack ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the FitTrack mobile application and web service.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate nutrition calculations, macro targets, and workout progress tracking, FitTrack collects the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and encrypted credentials when you register or authenticate via Google OAuth.
  • Fitness & Health Metrics: Age, weight, height, gender, workout frequency, and daily fitness goals.
  • Activity & Nutrition Tracking: Logged food items, calorie counts, macronutrient breakdowns (protein, carbs, fats), hydration logs, completed workout sets, and active calories burned.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To compute your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and customized daily caloric and macronutrient targets.
  • To synchronize your workout checklist, meal logs, and hydration counters across your devices in real-time.
  • To maintain and improve application performance and provide customer support.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is transmitted securely using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption. User account details and tracking records are stored in enterprise-grade, encrypted cloud databases (PostgreSQL / Supabase). We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third parties or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

FitTrack may integrate with trusted third-party providers:

  • Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0): For secure single sign-on authentication.
  • Supabase / Cloud Hosting: For reliable encrypted database storage and real-time synchronization.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may request the deletion of your account and all associated tracking data at any time by contacting us or using the in-app account management settings. Upon request, your account and historical records will be permanently purged from our database.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@fittrack.io
