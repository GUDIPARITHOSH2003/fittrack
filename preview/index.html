Download FitTrack

Standalone Mobile App

Install FitTrack directly onto your mobile phone's Home Screen & App Drawer with no app store required!

🤖 Android (Chrome)
  1. On your phone, open: http://192.168.1.119:8080
  2. Tap the "Install App Now" button below, OR tap Chrome's ⋮ (three dots) menu.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
🍎 iPhone (Safari)
  1. On your iPhone, open: http://192.168.1.119:8080
  2. Tap the Share button at bottom (square with arrow up 📤).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
